Witnessory

Run a probe

Help measure how far the internet has moved to post-quantum cryptography, from your own network. Your probe checks which key exchange public servers choose, sends back signed results, and adds your network to a view of the transition that no single vantage point can give. It gets its own public page and a place on the probe map, with its network and country (never its address).

Install

Linux · Raspberry Pi
curl -fsSL https://witnessory.org/install.sh | sudo sh

A system user, its own Python environment, a sandboxed service that starts at boot.

Windows
irm https://witnessory.org/install.ps1 -OutFile install.ps1
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File install.ps1

For your user, no administrator rights; runs every hour without a window. Needs Python 3.10+.

Docker
docker run -d --restart unless-stopped \
  --stop-timeout 120 -v witnessory-probe:/data \
  ghcr.io/witnessory/probe

The volume keeps your probe's keys and results.

Python
pipx install https://github.com/witnessory/probe/releases/download/v0.4.2/witnessory_probe-0.4.2-py3-none-any.whl
witnessory-probe start

Or try it first, with nothing stored: witnessory-probe check example.org

Read the installers before running them: install/. To remove it: curl -fsSL https://witnessory.org/install.sh | sudo sh -s -- --uninstall (or --purge, which also deletes the probe's keys and results); on Windows, run the installer with -Uninstall or -Purge.

What it does on your network

  • Only published measurements: a TLS key-exchange check on port 443 and an SSH key-exchange check on port 22. A task chooses which public sites to measure, never what to run.
  • Public servers only. It never connects to private, local or reserved addresses, or to its own IPv6 subnet.
  • Nothing about you. It reads how servers are configured, never your traffic or devices.
  • Light, and yours to control. At most 600 sites an hour by default; pause, limits, opt-outs and a log of every task.
  • Tamper-evident. Every result is signed by your probe (Ed25519 and ML-DSA-65) and chained to the one before.

Your probe

witnessory-probe me shows your probe's results and credits, and its public page, which shows its results and credits and never where it runs. Credits record what your probe has contributed; they are meant to become usable for Witnessory's services, such as checking your own sites from probes on many networks.
