Privacy

Witnessory measures servers, never people. This page says what our sites and services receive about you, and what they keep. Short version: we keep no record of who visits or what they look up.

This site (witnessory.org)

  • It is a static page hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your address and browser details, as any web host does, under GitHub's privacy statement. Witnessory receives nothing from your visit.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking of any kind by Witnessory.

The site checker (check.witnessory.org)

  • Requests pass through Cloudflare, which receives your address as any web host does, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • The checker uses your address only in memory, to limit how many new checks one address can start in an hour. It is never written down, logged or kept; it is gone within the hour or when the service restarts.
  • The site you ask about and its result are kept in memory for 10 minutes, so the next person asking gets the same signed result. Nothing links them to you.
  • We count requests by kind (for example "checked", "too many", "invalid name"), with no address or other detail, to see where the service fails people.

Counts we keep about our own services

To find and fix failures we look at Cloudflare's aggregate counts of requests to our services by site, path and status code (for example "12 requests to /api/check answered 503 last hour"). They contain no addresses and nothing about any visitor.
